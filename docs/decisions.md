# Decisions (these override docs/mvp-plan.md where they conflict)

1. Repo: single Next.js app with folders mirroring spec section 42 (workers/, data/, docs/ at the root; shared code under src/). Not a monorepo.
2. Admin: route group inside the web app, behind authentication on every page and API route.
3. PostGIS: yes, from the start.
4. Reset statuses: keep all 8 from spec section 4, plus the dimension fields.
5. License: [MIT / Apache 2.0]
6. Review policy: all AI-extracted events require human review at launch. Log every
   approve/edit/reject with source and event type so accuracy can be measured. Revisit
   auto-publishing for structured official sources once there is data.
7. Taxonomy: 24 types as listed in spec section 25.
8. Workers language: TypeScript, not Python (overrides spec section 43). Workers live under workers/ and run with tsx, sharing Node, pg, and tooling with the web app. Decided at step 2.1; Python was not installed and one toolchain is simpler to maintain.
9. No automated Telegram or Discord collection. Telegram's Content Licensing and AI Scraping terms and API terms §1.5 prohibit automated aggregation and AI use of platform data; Discord forbids self-bots and only allows bots invited by a server's admins. No Telegram migration, collector, or manual lead form is to be built.
10. Tier 4 publishing rule (for the review/approve step; to implement later): an event whose only SUPPORTS sources are Tier 4 cannot be published. It needs at least one Tier 1-3 supporting source.
11. Positions from Tier 4 sources: when a Tier 4 source reports a precise or current position, set location precision to District or Region, remove coordinates, and hold publication for 72 hours. Positions reported by official sources or established media can be shown as the source reported them.
12. Contradiction detection (MVP step 2.4) stays a reviewer action at launch: the reviewer sets `contradiction_flag`, its notes, and CONTRADICTS sources. Revisit when volume exceeds about 20 approvals a day, or after a contradiction is missed.
13. Confidence assessment (MVP step 2.5) is covered by the reviewer's choice at approval. The approve form shows a rule-based suggestion from the attached sources' tiers and types (no AI call); the reviewer decides.
14. Map dots (amends spec section 60 for the regional map only; requested and approved by the project owner). The map may draw one dot per area (a country, Kaliningrad Oblast, western Russia, the Baltic Sea, the Gulf of Finland) or per admin-1 region, at a fixed, hand-set anchor stored in data/map-anchors.json. Anchors are chosen once, in open land or sea away from towns and known military sites, rounded to 0.1 degrees, and never derived from, moved by, or jittered for events. Only the dot's size and colour change, in three count steps on an amber-to-orange heat scale (src/lib/map-style.ts, HEAT_SCALE; no red). The scale shows reporting volume, not intensity of activity, and the legend keeps the notes "Each dot marks an area, not a location." and "Counts reflect reporting, not intensity of activity." A dot marks an area, not a location. No per-event markers, no centroids of events, and no event coordinates reach the client. The rule against showing precise or current positions is unchanged, and the 72-hour exclusion of items supported only by Tier 4 sources still applies to the map. Items about places outside the theater are counted in cards beside the map, with no dots.
15. NATO content in the historical record (approved by the project owner, 2026-10-03). NATO's "Use of NATO content and brand" page (nato.int/en/about-us/official-texts-and-resources/use-of-nato-content-and-brand) allows credited reuse: "All content taken from NATO and republished must be clearly credited or sourced to NATO."; "Photos, videos and articles are released under the legally recognized terms of 'Fair Use' to members of the press, academia, non-profits and the general public."; "Material is provided, free of charge, for use in objective and balanced content, even if at times the end products may be critical of NATO."; "No material produced by NATO is to be sold, used for outside advertising or promotional purposes of any kind."; "NATO reserves the right to request the removal of NATO copyrighted material from any externally created content." The page is silent on automated access and AI use. Historical NATO articles are processed on the same basis as the live NATO feed, on these conditions: text articles only (no photos, video or multimedia); every NATO event credits NATO as its source; only a short excerpt and our own summary are stored; nothing is used for promotion or sale; and any NATO removal request is honoured.
16. Full-text fetching and new sources (rule set by the project owner, 2026-10-04; terms and robots.txt read 2026-10-04). A source's article pages are fetched (full_text in data/sources/collector.json), or a new source is collected, only when robots.txt permits our client and Anthropic's crawlers (ClaudeBot, anthropic-ai, Claude-User, Claude-SearchBot) on the paths fetched, and its terms do not forbid automated access, scraping, text mining or AI use (silence is acceptable). Skipped: any explicit prohibition, a robots.txt that blocks our client or Anthropic's crawlers or cannot be read, the no-AI, citation-only, blocked or prohibited lists, and sanctioned or blocked outlets (Vzglyad, RIA Novosti, Interfax, Kommersant). Terms limited to personal use, or requiring permission to republish, go to the owner for a decision and stay off. Decisions: Polish Ministry of National Defence (gov.pl), full text enabled: "Treści tekstowe publikowane w serwisie … są udostępniane na licencji … CC BY-SA 4.0" (gov.pl/web/gov/prawa-autorskie). Government Security Centre (RCB, gov.pl), enabled with full text: same gov.pl licence. CERT.LV, enabled with full text: no mention (no terms page; only privacy, cookie and disclosure policies). Defence24, needs owner decision: "solely within the limits of personal fair use permitted by law." Euronews, needs owner decision: "for your personal, non-commercial home use only". ERR, needs owner decision: "Materjalide igasugune kasutamine … ERR-i loata on keelatud" (any use without ERR's permission is forbidden, apart from personal non-commercial use). Rzeczpospolita (rp.pl), needs owner decision: "jedynie w ramach dozwolonego użytku osobistego" (personal permitted use only). 15min.lt, needs owner decision: use "tik savo asmeniniais ir informaciniais tikslais" (personal and informational purposes only); reprinting needs prior written consent. LSM, stays metadata only: "Interneta vietnē ievietoto saturu nedrīkst lejupielādēt, kopēt" (content may not be downloaded or copied). Jauns.lv, skipped and added to tos_prohibited_domains: forbids "indeksēšanu … kopēšanu … izmantošanu … pētījumos, analītiskos darbos" (indexing, copying, use in research and analysis). Vakarų ekspresas (ve.lt), skipped and added to blocked_automated_access: robots.txt disallows ClaudeBot and anthropic-ai. Lithuania's National Crisis Management Centre (lrvk.lrv.lt), skipped and added to blocked_automated_access: HTTP 403 to our client, including robots.txt. Polish Armed Forces Operational Command (wojsko-polskie.pl), skipped: robots.txt unreachable (connection reset, twice). DVIDS, skipped: robots.txt returned HTTP 502 on three attempts. U.S. Army Europe and Africa, U.S. Naval Forces Europe-Africa and USAFE-AFAFRICA: robots.txt and terms pass ("Information presented on this service … is considered public information and may be distributed or copied"; byline credit requested), but no site-specific feed was found; the shared RSS endpoint returns service-wide news, so nothing is collected. Belarus Ministry of Defence: full text off, at the owner's request; its feed carries titles only, and no earlier decision covers it.
17. Full text for five sources whose terms limit use to personal use (the project owner's decision, 2026-10-04; overrides the "needs a decision" outcome of decision 16 for these five only). Terms read 2026-10-04: Defence24, "The Service Recipient is entitled to use such content solely within the limits of personal fair use permitted by law." (defence24.com/term-of-use). Euronews, "You may download one copy of the materials on any single computer for your personal, non-commercial home use only" (euronews.com/terms-and-conditions). ERR, "Materjalide igasugune kasutamine (reprodutseerimine, töötlemine, avalik esitamine, eksponeerimine, üldsusele suunamine mistahes tehnilise vahendi kaudu, levitamine) ERR-i loata on keelatud." (info.err.ee/982667/kasutustingimused-ja-kommenteerimine). Rzeczpospolita (rp.pl), "Usługobiorca ma prawo do korzystania z nich jedynie w ramach dozwolonego użytku osobistego." (rp.pl/regulamin); Free items only (pay_status Free; Paid and Preview never). 15min.lt, "Jūs neturite teisės naudoti Turinio … išskyrus naudojimą išimtinai tik savo asmeniniais ir informaciniais tikslais." (15min.lt/naudojimosi-taisykles); 15min is registered and collected from 15min.lt/rss with Lithuanian keyword and region stems. Basis: the project is open-source, non-commercial and educational; from each article only our own summary, a supporting excerpt of at most 20 words and a link to the original are stored or shown; full article text is kept privately in raw_documents for extraction and never published; any removal request from a publisher is honoured. Still subject to robots.txt allowing our client on the paths fetched (enforced by HttpClient), the per-run page cap, and the skip lists. Jauns.lv, ve.lt, LSM, Lithuania's National Crisis Management Centre, and every no-AI, citation-only, blocked or prohibited source stay off.
18. AiF-Kaliningrad (klg.aif.ru), registered 2026-10-04 as a Russian state-aligned outlet (Argumenty i Fakty's Kaliningrad edition). Not one of the blocked outlets named in decision 16, and not found among the outlets under EU broadcasting bans checked on 2026-10-04 (national blocks in Lithuania and Latvia not verified). robots.txt (klg.aif.ru and aif.ru) allows our client and Anthropic's crawlers. Terms (site notice, aif.ru/static/1965027): "Копирование и использование полных материалов запрещено, частичное цитирование возможно только при условии гиперссылки на сайт www.aif.ru." (copying full materials is forbidden; partial quotation only with a hyperlink). So no full text is fetched; its RSS feed (klg.aif.ru/rss/all.php) carries titles only, collected with a region filter on titles, which makes its rows metadata-only leads that never reach the extractor. Tier 3 (spec section 28, journalism; the spec has no tier for state-aligned media) with reliability LOW and type ESTABLISHED_MEDIA; not an official source, so it carries no "State / official source" label. Any claim from it must be attributed to AiF-Kaliningrad and never stated as fact.
